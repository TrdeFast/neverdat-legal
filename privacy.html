Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

NeverDat Auto Repost is designed to run locally on the owner's Windows computer.

The application may process video links, local video files, publication status, and authentication credentials required to connect supported platforms.

Local configuration and processing records are stored on the owner's computer. Credentials should be kept private and should not be uploaded to public repositories.

When a platform is connected, its own privacy policy and data practices apply to information sent to that platform.
